# **Introduction to graph-based learning and data integration**

### *Author: Prof. Luis Romero Ben*

This 3-hour practical session is designed to complement the lecture slides from the course. The main objectives are:

*   **Construct algebraic graph operators** ($\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{\Omega}, \mathbf{D}, \mathbf{\Phi}, \mathbf{L}, \mathbf{B}$), verifying their properties at a simple example.
*   **Implement and analyse Graph Signal Processing (GSP) operators**, namely graph signal shifting, graph signal (edge) difference, local signal deviation, and global signal smoothing (Dirichlet energy minimization), over the simple example.
*   **Introduce the modelling of water distribution networks (WDN) as graphs** in Python through a realistic benchmark (**Modena**), reviewing graph operators and signals over a real-world case study.
*   **Generate hydraulic datasets** in Python, enabling the simulation of realistic conditions and scenarios in WDNs.
*   **Implement and compare different GNN architectures** against non-graph baselines water-related node, edge and graph-level tasks.
